# Service Area Page Rewrite Template
## Based on Daly City gold standard (ID 3954, rewritten 2026-05-30)

Use this structure for every service_areas page rewrite. Target: 1000–1400 words.

---

## H1 — Page Title
`Appliance Repair in [Neighborhood], San Francisco`

## Opening paragraph (100–120 words)
Describe the neighborhood: character, housing type, typical appliances, resident profile. Make it feel written by someone who knows the area.

**Example (Daly City):**
> Daly City sits just south of San Francisco, home to a mix of single-family homes, condos, and multi-unit buildings that house some of the largest appliance inventories in the Bay Area. Many homes here were built in the 1950s–70s — meaning older washers, dryers, and refrigerators that need maintenance, not just replacement. Smart Solution Appliances serves Daly City with same-day repair for all major brands.

---

## Section 1: Our Services in [Neighborhood] (200–250 words)

List top 5–6 services with 2–3 sentences each:
- Washer Repair
- Dryer Repair
- Refrigerator Repair
- Dishwasher Repair
- Oven / Range Repair
- (Optional) Freezer Repair

Each entry: what breaks, how we fix it, why not to wait.

---

## Section 2: Why [Neighborhood] Residents Choose Us (150 words)

3–4 bullet points:
- Same-day and next-day appointments
- All major brands (Samsung, LG, GE, Whirlpool, Bosch, Maytag, Kenmore, Sub-Zero)
- Flat-rate diagnostics — no surprise charges
- Licensed technicians, 90-day warranty on parts

---

## Section 3: Service Area Coverage (80–100 words)

List the zip code(s) served. Mention nearby neighborhoods we also serve (with internal links).

---

## Section 4: FAQ (3–5 questions, 50–80 words each)

Write questions that real customers in this neighborhood would ask. Tailor to housing type.

**Example questions:**
- "How quickly can you come to [Neighborhood]?"
- "Do you repair older [brand] appliances?"
- "What does appliance repair typically cost in San Francisco?"
- "Is it worth repairing a 10-year-old washer?"
- "Do you service apartment buildings in [Neighborhood]?"

---

## Closing CTA (60–80 words)

End with a clear call to action. Include phone number: **(415) 728-4163**.

> Ready to get your appliance fixed today? Smart Solution Appliances provides fast, reliable appliance repair throughout [Neighborhood] and all of San Francisco. Call **(415) 728-4163** or book online. Most repairs completed same day or next day.
